3. FULL DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 characters — current: ~1080)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kapilla Group Limited is a Tanzanian transport and general supply company
moving cargo by road, sea and air across Tanzania and more than 200
destinations worldwide.

This app keeps the essentials in one place:

• Track a consignment — enter the waybill number printed on your receipt to
  see the latest status and location of your cargo.
• Request a pickup — book a collection and our team confirms collection and
  routing.
• Freight solutions — road, sea and air freight, plus door-to-door delivery.
• General supply — office stationery, IT equipment, industrial tools, safety
  gear and building materials sourced and delivered.
• Our services — express courier, freight transportation, customs clearing
  and forwarding, ADC express, real-time tracking and a global network.
• Support — reach the team from the Help Center at any time.

Kapilla Group staff can also sign in to the staff portal to manage shipments,
invoices, clients and reports.

Reliable logistics, competitive pricing, and one partner for your supply
chain.
